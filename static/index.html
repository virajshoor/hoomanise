How it works

1. Submit

POST your text to /v1/humanize with a preset (casual, professional, academic) and an intensity from 20 to 90.

2. Rewrite

Up to seven candidate rewrites are generated, then ranked for meaning preservation, grammar, structural difference and natural style.

3. Collect

Poll the job or stream it over SSE. You get the rewritten text plus a style score showing how much the AI signal dropped.

What it actually changes

Most "humanizers" swap synonyms. That fails modern detectors. Hoomanise attacks the signals detectors actually read:

Sentence structure

Clause merging and splitting, active/passive switching, adverbial fronting, sentence reordering — only where meaning is preserved.

Rhythm & burstiness

Human writing mixes short, medium and long sentences. AI output is uniform. We vary it naturally, without manufacturing fragments.

AI phrasing

Stock tells like "it is important to note", "delves into", "in today's fast-paced landscape", "a testament to" are removed or rewritten.

Hard safety gates

Every candidate passes a meaning check (numbers, names, negations, claims), a grammar check, and a lexical-diversity check. Failures are rejected and retried.

Your formatting

Markdown, bullets, headings, URLs, code blocks, citations and acronyms are protected and restored intact.

Register control

Academic stays academic. Casual sounds casually human. Register is set by preset, independent from rewrite depth.

FAQ

How do I humanize AI text so it doesn't get flagged?

Rewrite the structure, not just the vocabulary. Detectors read sentence rhythm, predictability and stock phrasing. Hoomanise rebuilds sentences and rhythm and removes AI tells, while a meaning gate stops any rewrite that changes a claim. Start with the free credits and score your text with /v1/analyze before and after.

Is "humanise" the same as "humanize"?

Yes — British and American spellings of the same thing. The API, presets and results are identical.

How is this different from a paraphraser?

Paraphrasers swap words and keep the structure, which detectors see straight through. Hoomanise rebuilds sentence structure and rhythm while a hard meaning gate blocks any rewrite that changes a claim.

Will it change my meaning?

No — meaning preservation is the top constraint. Numbers, dates, names, negations and content words must survive, or the candidate is rejected and a safer rewrite is used. If no safe rewrite exists, your text is left essentially untouched.

Does it keep my formatting and citations?

Yes. Preserving formatting is on by default: Markdown, lists, headings, URLs, code, equations, (Author, year) citations and [1] references come through unchanged.

Which registers are supported?

Casual, professional and academic. Register is controlled by the preset and stays independent of intensity, so an academic paper never picks up slang.

Can I use it programmatically?

Yes — create API keys in the dashboard. Keys are shown once and stored only as a hash. Webhooks, batch submission and SSE streaming are available for automation.

What happens when my free credits run out?
